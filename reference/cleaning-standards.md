# Cleaning standards

How the cleaning is done at Calder House. Your mandatory training in Workday comes first. This is the site version
on top.

## The golden rules

1. **Only use chemicals you've been shown,** from labelled bottles, at the dilution on the label. **Never mix
   chemicals.** Not even two toilet cleaners.
2. **Chemicals live in the COSHH cupboard** in your floor's cleaning room. **Never leave them unattended on the
   floor,** even for a minute. **Lock the cupboard at the end of every shift.**
3. **Gloves on** for toilets and anything with chemicals.
4. **Wet floor sign out before you mop,** and leave it until the floor's dry.
5. **Colour-coded cloths and mops.** They never cross over:
   - **Red:** bathrooms and toilets
   - **Blue:** general areas: offices, desks, meeting and training rooms
   - **Green:** kitchens
6. **Don't move papers or touch screens and keyboards on desks.** If a desk is covered, wipe round it.
7. **Confidential paper** (the locked grey bins) is never opened.

## The job list

Each floor's cleaning room has a **laminated list** of the jobs for that floor. That list is the standard. If
this file and the list disagree, go with the list and tell Dean Okafor.

This site is busier than most. **Everything below is done every shift,** including fridges, microwaves and
hoovering. If there are only two of you on a floor, you'll need to manage your four hours carefully to get through
it all. If you can see you won't finish, tell Dean before the end of the shift, not after.

## Your round, every shift

**Offices and training rooms**

- Empty every bin and recycling bin. New liner in each general waste bin.
- Wipe clear desk space, meeting tables and door handles.
- Hoover.
- Training rooms: wipe tables, clear cups and food, wipe the whiteboards.

**Kitchens** (green)

- Load and start the dishwasher. If it's full and clean, empty it first.
- Wipe worktops, sinks and taps.
- Clean the microwave, inside and out.
- Clean the fridge, and bin anything past its date.
- Mop the floor last.

**Toilets** (red)

- Clean toilets, urinals, sinks, taps, mirrors and surfaces.
- Restock toilet roll, hand towels and soap from the cleaning room.
- Mop the floor last, working back towards the door.

## Before you leave a floor

- Chemicals back in the COSHH cupboard, and the cupboard locked.
- Kitchen taps off, dishwasher running or done.
- Trolley with you. Never leave it on a floor.
- Anything broken, leaking or out of stock: tell Dean Okafor before the end of the shift.

## Who checks the standards

Marcus Bell, the client's facilities manager, keeps an eye on the cleaning. If something's been missed, he tells
Dean Okafor, and Dean passes it on to you. Everyone gets feedback in their first few weeks. It's not a
telling-off.
