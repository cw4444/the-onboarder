# Pay and clocking in

How your hours get recorded, and what has to be sorted so you actually get paid.

## The golden rule for pay problems

**Take anything about pay, clocking or Workday to Dean Okafor first.** Don't email Workday, HR, payroll or
Timegate yourself. Dean knows who to send it to.

The one exception is your **P45**, which you send yourself through a Workday case (see below).

## Clocking in and out: Timegate

Clocking in is done on **Timegate**. It records your hours for pay, and it's also the safety check: it's how the
company knows you're on site, and that you've left safely. So **clock out at the end of every shift, every day.**

**Setting up the Timegate app:**

1. Download **Timegate** from the App Store or Google Play.
2. Log in with the details in the email you got from Timegate.
3. No email? Tap **Forgot password** and use your **personal email address** (the one you gave when you applied).

**To clock in on the app:** open it at the start of your shift and enter the **site ID number**. You don't need
your employee number for the app. Dean Okafor gives you the site ID on your first shift.

**To clock out on the app:** just press the clock-off button at the end of your shift.

**No app, or it's not working? Use the phone line instead:**

1. Ring the Timegate number: **0161 496 0800**. (fictional number)
2. Press **1** to clock on, then key in your **employee number**. You'll find it in Workday, on your profile.
3. To clock off, ring the same number at the end of your shift, press **2**, and key in your employee number.

**If neither the app nor the phone line works,** don't keep trying and don't just start without it. Tell Dean
Okafor straight away. Dean can check your details and log your times manually.

**Forgot to clock in or out?** Tell Dean Okafor the same shift, so Dean can put your times in.

**Never clock in or out for someone else,** even a friend who's running late.

## Your pay

- You're paid **£14 an hour.**
- You're paid **monthly, on the last day of the month**, even though you're paid by the hour.
- The **payroll cut-off is the 15th of the month.** Anything that affects your pay (your P45, bank details, missed
  clocking) needs to be sorted by then to make that month's pay.
- Your payslips are in **Workday**, under **Pay → Payslips**.

## What's a P45?

A P45 is the form your last employer gives you when you leave a job. It tells your new employer how much you've
earned and paid in tax so far this tax year, so they can put you on the right tax code. Without it, you're likely
to go on an emergency tax code and pay too much tax until it's sorted.

## Before your first payday

Do these in your first week. Lots of people miss the first one, and it's the one that costs you money.

1. **Send your P45.** Raise a case in Workday using this link:
   **https://lindengray.example.com/workday/p45-case**. Upload a photo of your P45 and send it. This is the only time you
   contact payroll directly.
   **No P45?** Fill in the government's **starter checklist** online instead:
   **https://www.gov.uk/guidance/starter-checklist-for-paye**. It gets you on the right tax code, so you don't end
   up on emergency tax.
2. **Change your Workday password.** The one in your welcome email is temporary. Change it to your own straight
   away.
3. **Bank details.** Put them into Workday, under **Pay → Payment Elections**. Use an account in your own name.
   **Urgent if it's not done by the cut-off.**
4. **National Insurance number.** Check it's in Workday under your personal information. If you don't know it,
   it's on old payslips or a P60. If you've never had one, tell Dean Okafor.

## Questions about your pay

- **Pay looks wrong, or didn't arrive:** tell Dean Okafor on your next shift. Dean takes it to payroll.
- **Tax questions, like an emergency tax code:** tell Dean Okafor, who'll get payroll to look at it.
- **Overtime or holiday pay:** it's in your contract. Ask Dean Okafor if something doesn't match.
