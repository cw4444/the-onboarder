# When things go wrong

What to do, and who to tell. If you're ever not sure whether something's serious, treat it as serious and tell
Dean Okafor.

## Stop and tell Dean Okafor straight away

Don't try to sort these yourself. Stop what you're doing, make it safe if you can do that without touching
anything, and go and find Dean. If you can't find Dean, ring the supervisor phone (07700 900123). If someone's in
danger, ring **999** first.

- **You've hurt yourself,** even a small cut or a twinge in your back.
- **Chemical in your eyes, on your skin, or you've breathed something in.** Rinse with water first, then tell Dean.
- **You've mixed two chemicals by mistake,** or a bottle smells odd. Leave the room, shut the door, tell Dean.
- **A needle or anything sharp** in a bin or a toilet. Don't touch it. Put a wet floor sign by it and tell Dean.
- **Blood, sick or other body fluids.** Don't clean it up with your normal kit. Tell Dean.
- **Someone makes you feel unsafe,** whether it's one of Blackthorne & Blackthorne's staff, a visitor or anyone else.
- **Someone in the building who shouldn't be.** Don't confront them. Tell Dean.

The site has trained first aiders, and every floor has a notice saying who they are. You won't know them yet, so
**always alert Dean first**. Anything that happens to you at work
goes in the **accident book** in B04. Dean helps you fill it in.

## Fire alarm

- **Continuous alarm:** leave by the nearest fire exit. Leave your trolley. Don't use the lifts. Go to the
  assembly point in the car park across Mill Lane and find Dean, so you can be ticked off the list.
- **Wednesday 6pm test:** a short ring. Carry on.

## Running late or can't come in

- **Running late:** ring Dean on the supervisor phone (07700 900123) as soon as you know.
- **Off sick:** **ring** Dean on the supervisor phone before your shift. **No texts.** You don't need to log it
  anywhere. It gets logged for you.

## Problems with the building or kit

- **Leak, flood, broken toilet, lights out:** put a wet floor sign out if there's water, and tell Dean.
- **Broken hoover or kit:** stop using it, put a "do not use" note on it, and tell Dean. Don't try to fix it,
  especially anything electrical.
- **A door that won't lock,** especially the outside door or the adjoining door by the bins: don't leave it. Tell
  Dean straight away.
- **Something of Blackthorne & Blackthorne's is broken** (you knocked a mug off a desk, say): it happens. Tell Dean the same
  shift. Honesty is always better than someone finding it in the morning.
- **Found something valuable** (phone, wallet, keys): hand it to Dean.

## Pay and clocking problems

These feel less urgent than an injury, but they can cost you money. See `pay-and-clocking-in.md`.

- **Timegate not working, or you forgot to clock in or out:** tell Dean the same shift. Dean can log your times.
- **Pay is wrong or didn't arrive:** tell Dean. Don't email payroll, HR, Workday or Timegate yourself.

## Something's not right at work

- **Any issue at all:** start with Dean Okafor, evening supervisor.
- **A problem with Dean:** go to Marcus Bell, Blackthorne & Blackthorne's facilities manager.
- **You don't want to talk to either of them:** go to Priya Shah, area manager.

Contact details are in `whos-who.md`.
