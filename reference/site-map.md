# Site map

Where things are in Calder House, Blackthorne & Blackthorne's headquarters. It's two buildings, **Lotus** and
**Yew**. This map is Lotus, where you'll start. Rooms are numbered by floor: B04 is in the basement,
3.12 is on floor 3.

There's a **"you are here" map in every room**, and a **first aider notice on each floor**. If you're lost, look
for the map on the wall.

## Getting in

- **Staff entrance:** the goods entrance at the back, on Mill Lane. You swipe in with your pass.
- **First shift, no pass yet:** text or ring Dean Okafor on the supervisor phone (07700 900123) when you arrive,
  and Dean will come and collect you.
- **Security desk:** at the front. You sign the cleaning cupboard keys out and back in there. You don't sign
  yourself in: Timegate is your check-in.
- **Parking:** on site, next to the building. Use any space that isn't marked **Reserved**. The electric vehicle
  charging spaces are at the rear.
- **Don't use** the main doors at the front to come in. They're for visitors and Blackthorne & Blackthorne's
  staff.

## Basement

- **B04, the cleaners' store:** your base. All the supplies, the trolleys, the first aid box and the accident book.
  Dean Okafor starts and ends the shift here.
- **B05, the break room:** next door to B04. Kettle, fridge, microwave, lockers. Bring your own padlock.
- **Outside bins:** through the adjoining door at the end of the basement corridor, then the outside door. **Green
  for general waste, blue for mixed recycling, the locked grey one for confidential paper.**
- **Every time you come back in from the bins, check both the outside door and the adjoining door are firmly
  locked.**

## Ground floor

- Main reception, the **security desk** and the **canteen**.
- The **goods / service lift** goes from the canteen area down to the basement. Use it with your trolley. Don't take
  trolleys in the main lifts.
- Toilets by the main lifts.

## Floors 1 to 5

Every floor has the same layout: open-plan offices, a kitchen, and toilets (ladies, gents and an accessible one),
all off the main corridor by the lifts.

- **Floor 2** is mostly **training rooms** (2.01 to 2.08). Check the booking screen outside before you go in.
- **Cleaning room** on each floor, next to the toilets, numbered x.20 (so 3.20 on floor 3). It has:
  - the **COSHH cupboard**, where the chemicals live. Lock it at the end of every shift.
  - the **laminated job list** for that floor.
  - spare bin bags, toilet roll, hand towels and soap.

## Floor 6

Directors' offices and Blackthorne & Blackthorne's server room. **The cleaning team doesn't go on floor 6** unless Dean Okafor
says so.

## Yew, the other building

Linden & Gray Facilities cleans both buildings. **Yew** runs 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Dean Okafor will tell you if you're moving, and show you round the first time.

## In an emergency

- **Fire assembly point:** the car park across Mill Lane, by the bike shelter. Go there even if it turns out to be
  a drill.
- **Fire exits:** at both ends of every corridor. Green signs. Never prop them open or block them with a trolley.
- **Fire alarm test:** Wednesdays at 6pm. If the alarm goes at any other time, it's real.
