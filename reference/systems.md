## Timegate

For clocking in and out. It's also your **safety check-in**: it's how the company knows who's on site, so clock
out every shift.

- Download **Timegate** from the App Store or Google Play.
- Log in with the details in your Timegate email. No email? Tap **Forgot password** and use your personal email.
- To clock in on the app you need the **site ID number**, not your employee number. To clock out, press the
  clock-off button.
- If the app won't work, there's a phone line. Steps are in `pay-and-clocking-in.md`.

## Workday

Linden & Gray Facilities' HR system. You use it for:

- **Your profile:** your employee number (you need this for the Timegate phone line, not the app) and personal information,
  including your National Insurance number.
- **Pay → Payslips:** your payslips. No paper ones.
- **Pay → Payment Elections:** your bank details.
- **Learning:** your mandatory training. There's a lot. Start it in your first week.
- **Cases:** only for sending your P45, through the link in `pay-and-clocking-in.md`.

**Logging in for the first time:**

1. Your login details come in a **welcome email**. Check your junk folder.
2. Log in with the temporary password from that email.
3. **Change the password to your own straight away.** Nobody on site knows your password, and nobody will ever ask
   you for it.

If you haven't had the email, or the password doesn't work, tell Dean Okafor.

## Your rota

Your hours are in your **contract** and stay the same. Evenings are **4pm to 8pm, Monday to Friday.**

What changes is **where** you work. Dean Okafor moves people between floors, and between buildings on the site,
so everyone gets experience everywhere. That includes the building that runs **24 hours a day, 7 days a week.**

There's a small screen outside each training room showing if it's booked. If it says **"In use"**, skip that room
and come back later. Rooms are often booked into the evening.
