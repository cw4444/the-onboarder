# Systems

The apps you'll use, and your pass. The details of clocking in and pay are in `pay-and-clocking-in.md`.

**If any of these isn't working, tell Dean Okafor.** Don't email Workday or Timegate yourself. If Dean isn't answering, follow the reporting line in `whos-who.md`.

## Timegate

For clocking in and out. It's also your **safety check-in**: it's how the company knows who's on site, so clock
out every shift.

- Download **Timegate** from the App Store or Google Play.
- Log in with the details in your Timegate email. No email? Tap **Forgot password** and use your personal email.
- To clock in on the app you need the **site ID number**, not your employee number. To clock out, press the
  clock-off button.
- If the app won't work, there's a phone line. Steps are in `pay-and-clocking-in.md`.

## Workday

Linden & Gray Facilities' HR system. Tap **Menu** to open the sidebar. Everything below is in there.

- **Time & Absence:** booking time off. Open it and pick the option you need. **Can't see the menu?** It does
  happen. Type **manage absence** into the search bar instead.
- **Your profile:** your employee number (you need this for the Timegate phone line, not the app) and personal information,
  including your National Insurance number.
- **Pay → Payslips:** your payslips. No paper ones.
- **Pay → Payment Elections:** your bank details.
- **Learning:** your mandatory training. There's a lot. Start it in your first week.
- **Cases:** only for sending your P45, through the link in `pay-and-clocking-in.md`.

**Booking time off:**

1. **Ask Dean Okafor first** whether you can take it. Dean knows if anyone else is off.
2. Then request it in Workday, under **Menu → Time & Absence**.
3. Dean Okafor approves it once it's submitted.

**Logging in for the first time:**

1. Your login details come in a **welcome email**. Check your junk folder.
2. Log in with the temporary password from that email.
3. **Change the password to your own straight away.** Nobody on site knows your password, and nobody will ever ask
   you for it.

If you haven't had the email, or the password doesn't work, tell Dean Okafor.

## Your rota

Your hours are in your **contract** and stay the same. Evenings are **4pm to 8pm, Monday to Friday.**

What changes is **where** you work. Dean Okafor moves people between floors, and between buildings on the site,
so everyone gets experience everywhere. Calder House is two buildings, Lotus and Yew, and Yew runs **24 hours a day, 7 days a week.** Dean
will tell you when you're moving.

## Your pass

- Your pass is issued by Linden & Gray Facilities. Dean Okafor gives it to you on your first shift.
- You **swipe in** with it. It gets you in at reception, and opens the basement, and the floors you clean. It doesn't open
  floor 6.
- Until you've got it, text or ring Dean on the supervisor phone when you arrive, and Dean will collect you from
  reception. If Dean doesn't answer, follow "Getting in" in `site-map.md`.
- **Lost your pass?** Tell Dean Okafor the same shift.

## Keys

The cleaning room and COSHH cupboard on each floor are locked. The keys are **signed out with front desk security**
at the start of the shift, and **signed back in** at the end. Every shift, no exceptions.

## Wifi

Contractors **don't get access** to Blackthorne & Blackthorne's wifi. That includes the cleaning team.

## Training-room booking screens

There's a small screen outside each training room showing if it's booked. If it says **"In use"**, skip that room
and come back later. Rooms are often booked into the evening.
