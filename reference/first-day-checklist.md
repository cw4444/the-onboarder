# First-day checklist

Your first shift, in the order you'll need it. Evening shifts are **4pm to 8pm (16:00 to 20:00), Monday to
Friday.**

## Before your first shift

- **Right to work:** this is done before you start. You'll get an email from **Yoti** asking you to verify your
  ID on the **Post Office ID app**. Do it as soon as it arrives. You can't start until it's done.
- **Uniform:** you'll have had a text asking your sizes. You get your trousers and polo shirt on your
  first shift. You don't need to buy anything.
- **Shoes:** work shoes with **non-slip soles**. Floors get wet. **No Crocs.**
- **Apps:** download **Timegate** and log into **Workday** if you've had the emails. If you haven't, that's fine,
  Dean Okafor will help on your first shift. See `systems.md`.
- **Get ahead if you can:** change your Workday password, put your bank details in, and send your P45 (or fill in
  the starter checklist). They're all in "This week" below, but doing them now means one less thing on day one.
- **Arrive 10 minutes early,** so you've got time to find Dean and clock in.

## First hour

1. **Go to main reception at Calder House.** You won't have a pass to swipe in yet, so **text or ring the
   supervisor phone (07700 900123)** when you arrive. Dean Okafor will come and collect you from reception.
2. **Dean takes you down to the cleaners' store, room B04 in the basement.** That's your base. If you ever lose
   Dean, wait in the store. Don't go looking round the building on your own.
3. **Clock in on Timegate.** Dean will walk you through it the first time. How it works is in
   `pay-and-clocking-in.md`. If you can't clock in, Dean logs your start time by hand. Don't skip it either way.
4. **Get your kit.** Your uniform, your pass, and your trolley. Dean shows you which trolley is yours, and how to
   **sign out your floor's cleaning cupboard keys** with front desk security.
5. **Safety walk-round with Dean.** Fire exits, the fire assembly point, the first aid box, the accident book,
   the first aider notice on each floor (you won't know them yet, so if anyone's hurt, alert Dean first), and the COSHH cupboard in each floor's cleaning room. Ask if any get
   missed.

## Your first shift

- **You shadow Sandra Hughes on your first two shifts.** Sandra's been on the evening team for years. Dean
  introduces you at the start of your first shift.
- **You'll also do some work on your own,** so the jobs get done. Dean Okafor decides which bits you do alone. You
  won't have your own floor until Dean says so. If you're stuck, ask.
- **There's no set break.** Shifts are four hours, so breaks aren't required. If you need a few minutes, for a
  smoke or anything else, ask Dean.
- **Never use a chemical you haven't been shown.** If a bottle has no label, don't use it. Tell Dean.
- **Never leave chemicals on the floor unattended.** They go back in the locked COSHH cupboard.
- **If a room still has people in it, skip it and come back.** Don't ask them to leave. Tell Dean if they're still
  there at the end of your round.

## Before you go home

1. **Lock the chemicals away** in the COSHH cupboard on your floor, put your trolley back in B04, and **sign the
   keys back in** with front desk security.
2. **Take the bins out, then lock up behind you.** When you come back in from the bins, check **both** the outside
   door and the adjoining door are firmly locked.
3. **Clock out on Timegate.** Every shift, every day. It's how the company knows you've left safely, and how you
   get paid.
4. **Ask Dean about your P45.** Do you know how to send it? It's the thing new starters miss most, and it costs
   you money if you don't. See `pay-and-clocking-in.md`.

## This week

- **Send your P45** through the Workday link in `pay-and-clocking-in.md`. Do it before the payroll cut-off.
- **Change your Workday password** to your own. The one in the email is temporary.
- **Put your bank details into Workday.** See `pay-and-clocking-in.md`.
- **Start your mandatory training in Workday** as soon as you can. There's a lot of it, so don't leave it to the
  last minute.
- **Read the cleaning standards** (`cleaning-standards.md`) and the laminated job list in your floor's cleaning
  room. You don't need to learn it all at once.
